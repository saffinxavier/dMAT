# dMAT exam day & APS India — what you need to know

Official-first summary for learners sitting **Core + General Academic Module** (APS India path).  
Interactive copy: hub button **About dMAT** in the prep app.

**Primary sources:** [d-mat.de](https://www.d-mat.de/en/), [GAM prep PDF](https://www.d-mat.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/260716_dMAT_General-Academic-Module_Preparatoy-Materials_EN.pdf), [APS India dMAT](https://aps-india.de/dmat/).  
Dates / fee / centres can change — re-check official pages before you register.

---

## 1. What dMAT is

The **Digital Master Assessment Test** is a computer-based aptitude test (English) run by **g.a.s.t.**

It has two parts:

| Module | Role (APS India) |
| --- | --- |
| **Core Module** | General study aptitude — three timed subtests |
| **Subject Module** | **General Academic Module (GAM)** — academic scenarios + related questions |

It is **not** a syllabus cram exam and **not** a pass/fail gate by itself. Universities and APS use the certificate differently; check each programme.

---

## 2. Structure & timing

| Part | Items | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core — Figure Sequences (FS) | 20 series | **25 min** |
| Core — Mathematical Equations (ME) | 20 systems | **25 min** |
| Core — Latin Squares (LS) | 20 tasks | **25 min** |
| **Break** | — | **30 min** |
| General Academic Module | Scenario(s) + questions | **90 min** |

- Active testing ≈ **75 min Core + 90 min GAM**; with break ≈ **~3.5 hours** total (official: about three hours of exam work + 30 min break / “three and a half hours” overall).
- Counts/times above match the **official GAM preparatory PDF** (not third-party blogs that claim different LS counts).

---

## 3. What each part asks

| Subtest | In one line |
| --- | --- |
| **Figure Sequences** | Four matrices in a series; choose what continues (movement, rotation, colour, step size, edges). |
| **Mathematical Equations** | Letters = distinct integers **1–20**; find the asked value. |
| **Latin Squares** | 5×5 grid, letters **A–E**, once per row and column; find `?`. |
| **GAM** | Read a short academic problem (text / table / figure); answer related **single-choice** questions from **givens**, not memorised trivia. |

---

## 4. Exam-day rules (confirmed in official text)

- **Language:** English  
- **Format:** Computer-based, **single-choice** (one correct option)  
- **Notes:** **Not allowed** for the whole exam  
- **Calculator / helping tools:** Not provided; practice without notes  
- **Guessing:** Official instruction is to **guess** if you do not know (blank earns nothing; no penalty for wrong answers is stated)  
- **Detailed task instructions:** In prep materials / videos — exam day only shows short reminders  

---

## 5. Navigation & changing answers (honest)

Official PDFs stress timing, no notes, and guessing. They do **not** fully document every on-screen control in writing.

**Authoritative for UI:** watch the g.a.s.t. videos on  
[Preparation for the exam](https://www.d-mat.de/en/preparation-for-the-exam/)  
(Intro, FS, ME, LS, Subject).

**This repo’s Exam Mock approximates:**

- Move between items **within** a subtest; change answers while that subtest is open  
- **End subtest is irreversible** — you cannot return to a finished Core subtest  
- After Core → break → GAM  

Treat the mock as practice for flow, **not** a pixel-perfect clone.

## 6. Scoring & certificate

From [d-mat.de](https://www.d-mat.de/en/):

| Scale | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **dMAT score** | Correct answers converted to **0–200** (mean / average ≈ **100**) for Core and for Subject separately |
| **Total** | Core score + Subject score |
| **Percentile rank** | Share of all participants (all centres/dates) who scored lower or equal |
| **Pass mark** | None fixed by g.a.s.t.; universities decide how to use results |
| **Validity** | Certificate is **valid indefinitely**; download from the participant portal when results are out |

APS India attaches the dMAT certificate to APS documentation; it does **not** replace document verification or university admission.

## 7. APS India package (who / when / fee)

### Who must take it (introductory phase)

Applicants whose **previous degree** is in:

- Engineering  
- Commerce / Accounting / Finance / Economics  
- Business / Management  

…and who apply for a **Master** in Germany for **Summer Semester 2027 or later**, must take dMAT with the **General Academic Module** as part of APS documentation — unless an exemption applies.

See the official affected-fields list and FAQ: [aps-india.de/dmat](https://aps-india.de/dmat/).

### Typical exemptions (check APS wording)

Examples APS lists: Bachelor applicants; incomplete bachelor progress thresholds; PhD; fields outside the list; confirmed exchange/double-degree/partnership programmes; APS registration / document ship **before 29 June 2026**; already completed APS with certificate for that procedure.

### First India schedule (2026 cycle)

| Milestone | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Registration opens | 29 June 2026 |
| Registration deadline | **15 September 2026** |
| Exam | **26 September 2026** |
| Certificate online | **12 October 2026** |

### Fee & centres

- **Fee:** €150 (paid to g.a.s.t. at registration) — confirm on the portal  
- **Centres (planned):** Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Bhopal, Chandigarh, Chennai, Kolkata, Mananthavady, Mumbai, New Delhi, Pune, Kathmandu (Nepal). Final list appears during booking.  
- Register: [g.a.s.t. dMAT portal](https://www.gast.de/portal/center-search/center-search/dmat/exams/worldwide?lang=en)

### APS + dMAT workflow

- You may **submit APS documents before** the dMAT certificate is ready.  
- APS can only **issue the APS certificate** after the required dMAT certificate is submitted and checked against g.a.s.t.  
- dMAT does **not** replace anabin / degree recognition / university offers.
