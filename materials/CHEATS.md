## Figure Sequences (FS)

1. **MOVE** — Where does each figure go? (H / V / diagonal; no mid-run type switch for diagonals.)
2. **SPIN** — Rotation around its own axis (often 90°).
3. **HUE** — Colour cycle or flip.
4. **STEP** — Step size grows (1, then 2, then 3…).
5. **Edge** — Bounce back or slide along the border.
6. **NO-GHOST** — Nothing disappears; nothing overlaps.

Mnemonic: *“Move, spin, hue, step — no ghosts at the edge.”*

---

## Mathematical Equations (ME)

1. **PIN** — Find `A = 7` style or the equation with fewest unknowns.
2. **CHAIN** — Substitute into the next equation.
3. **1–20 UNIQUE** — Each letter a different integer from 1 to 20.
4. Out of time → plug remaining options into all equations; guess rather than leave blank if needed.

Mnemonic: *“Pin it, chain it, keep them unique.”*

---

## Latin Squares (LS)

1. **FAT ROW/COL FIRST** — Start where most cells are already filled.
2. **CROSS OUT** — In the `?` row and column, drop letters that already appear.
3. If two candidates remain, fill a neighbour cell mentally, then return.

Mnemonic: *“Fat first, then cross out.”*

---

## General Academic Module (GAM)

1. **GIVEN vs GUESS** — Numbers, limits, and “must / must not” in the text are given.
2. **KILL CONTRADICTIONS** — Delete any option that breaks a given.
3. Re-check tables/figures before locking the answer.

Mnemonic: *“Given first, kill contradictions.”*
