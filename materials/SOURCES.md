# dMAT / related prep sources

**Past papers:** There are **no official dMAT previous-year question papers**. The APS India sitting started with the 2026 cycle. Anything sold as “dMAT past papers” from before 2026 is not the real exam.

Use official prep PDFs first. This repo’s [`app/`](../app/) ships **original** practice items in the same formats (not copies of g.a.s.t. exercises).

---
